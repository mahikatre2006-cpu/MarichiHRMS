MarichiHR Enterprise Foundation

Core System Engines & Architecture Layers

An in-depth, layer-by-layer technical and operational breakdown of the six core pillars: Organization Structure, Employee Master, Single Sign-On, 5-Level RBAC, Attendance Engine, and Statutory Leave Ledger.

1

Organisation Hierarchy (The Skeleton)

Tiered Multi-Tenant Structure

Before adding staff or tracking attendance, the platform builds a four-tier corporate tree. Every data record is permanently anchored to this hierarchy, making cross-company data leakage physically impossible.

Tier 1 · Tenant

Organisation

The root tenant domain (e.g. Acme Group). Enforces strict database isolation across all collections.

Tier 2 · Legal

Legal Entity

Incorporated company (e.g. Acme India Pvt Ltd). Governs corporate currency (INR), tax jurisdiction, and fiscal year.

Tier 3 · Physical

Location / Branch

Physical workplace (e.g. Mumbai HQ). Holds GPS latitude, longitude, and geofence punch radius (e.g. 300m).

Tier 4 · Team

Department

Functional unit (e.g. Engineering, Sales). Linked to Department Heads for approval workflows.

2

Employee Master Data (The People)

Single Source of Truth

The Employee Master ties each person to their organizational position, employment terms, and reporting structure.

1. Permanent Identifiers

  • Employee Code: Immutable code (e.g., ACME-001).
  • Official Email: Used for authentication and notifications.
  • Designation & Joining Date: Determines seniority & tenure.

2. Managerial Tree (managerId)

  • Direct link to their supervisor's profile.
  • No Circular Loops: System prevents Person A from reporting to Person B if B reports to A.
  • Powers approval queues and live team presence.

3. Lifecycle & User Link

  • Status: ACTIVE, PROBATION, or TERMINATED.
  • Binds to login credentials and system roles.
  • Preserves immutable audit trail upon offboarding.
3

SSO & Authentication (Access Layer)

OpenID Connect + JWT

Zero password friction for employees, enterprise-grade identity federation for IT administrators.

The Canonical Callback Pattern One route for all providers
GET /api/v1/auth/sso/:provider/callback
• /sso/google/callback
• /sso/microsoft/callback
• /sso/okta/callback
• /sso/keycloak/callback
1. Provider Verification Google or Microsoft verifies corporate identity and returns a verified email token.
2. Employee Matching MarichiHR matches the email against the active Employee directory within the tenant.
3. Token Issuance Issues a signed 15-min Tenant JWT access token and a secure HttpOnly refresh cookie.
4

5-Level RBAC Scope Hierarchy (Security Gate)

Granular Authorization

A user's role grants permission to act, but their Scope determines whose records they are permitted to touch.

Level Scope Who Gets It? Operational Boundary
Level 1 SELF Staff Employee Can view and touch only their own attendance, punch clock, and leave balance.
Level 2 TEAM People Manager Direct and indirect reports down the managerial tree, plus active delegations.
Level 3 DEPARTMENT Department Head All employees within the assigned department (e.g. Engineering).
Level 4 ENTITY Country Director All staff across all branches of that specific legal corporation.
Level 5 ORGANISATION HR / Enterprise Admin Full tenant-wide access across all entities, branches, and departments.
5

Attendance Management Engine (Punches & Shifts)

Real-Time Verification

1. GPS Geofencing (Haversine Distance)

When clocking in, the system reads device coordinates and runs the Haversine formula against the branch's GPS coordinates. If within 300 meters, punch is verified.

2. Shift Calculation & Grace Period

Shift defined as 09:00 – 18:00 with a 15-minute morning grace. Punches up to 09:15 AM are marked "On Time". Break deductions apply only when worked time exceeds 4–5 hours.

3. Punch Regularisation Workflow

Missed a punch due to a client visit or biometric glitch? Employee submits correction request with reason. Manager approves $\rightarrow$ system automatically recalculates hours and status.

4. Month-End Attendance Lock

On the 30th/31st, HR Admin clicks Lock Attendance. All punch records for that month freeze into an immutable state. No edits allowed before payroll execution.

6

Leave Management Engine (Statutory Ledger)

Double-Entry Accounting

Treats employee leave balances with the exact same rigor as monetary bank ledgers:

Double-Entry Transaction Ledger (`LeaveLedgerEntry`)
• CREDIT: Annual Allocation (+18.0 days, Ref: #INIT-2026)
• DEBIT: Approved Vacation (-2.0 days, Ref: #REQ-9812)
• REVERT: Cancelled Request (+2.0 days restored, Ref: #CAN-9812)
• ADJUSTMENT: HR Comp Off Credit (+1.0 day, Ref: #ADJ-4412)
➔ Closing Available Balance = Sum of Verified Ledger Entries
10 Statutory Types Paid Leave (PL), Sick Leave (SL), Casual Leave (CL), Maternity, Paternity, Bereavement, Leave Without Pay (LWP), Comp Off, Sabbatical, Custom.
Attendance Auto-Sync When a manager approves leave, the system deducts days from the ledger AND sets daily attendance to ON_LEAVE.
Approval Delegation When a manager takes vacation, they assign temporary approval power to a peer manager for a set date range.
